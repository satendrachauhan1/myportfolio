SmartWare House Inventory Allocator

CSE • Atria I.T.

Problem Formulation, AI Objectives & Requirement Analysis

Problem Formulation & Industry Context

Warehouses frequently handle thousands of SKUs with fluctuating demand patterns. Traditional manual allocation leads to severe operational bottlenecks:

  • •Stockouts: Fast-moving products run out before replenishment due to delayed manual decisions.
  • •Overstocking: Slow-moving inventory occupies premium footprint, inflating storage costs.
  • •Space Inefficiency: Suboptimal physical zone utilization across storage tiers.
  • •Manual Latency: Raw spreadsheet audits slow down high-volume dispatch operations.

Core AI Objectives

01. Demand Prediction

Estimates future product velocity using historical sales telemetry and movement curves.

02. Priority Scoring

Ranks inventory dynamically based on current stock, urgency, sales velocity, and business rules.

03. Smart Allocation

Generates actionable recommendations for exact stock quantities to place in pick/pack zones or orders.

04. Anomaly Detection

Flags sudden demand spikes, unexpected stock drift, and stagnant inventory anomalies.

Proposed AI Solution Workflow

Raw Inventory Data→Cleaning→Demand Prediction→Priority Scoring→Allocation Logic→Human Approval

*System philosophy: Produces explainable allocation recommendations to augment staff decision-making rather than fully unmonitored execution.

System Requirement Specifications

CategorySpecification Breakdown
InputsProduct ID, category, stock level, sales history, demand trends, storage capacity, order priority.
AI OutputsRecommended allocation quantity, storage zone assignment, order priority queues, low-stock alerts.
Tech StackPython, Machine Learning algorithms, Relational/Document DB, Web-based user management dashboard.
